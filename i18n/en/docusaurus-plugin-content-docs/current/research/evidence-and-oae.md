# Evidence and OAE: carry the scope of a match

The Saitō school proposes a research mapping from Weaver's A (technical), B (semantic), and C (effectiveness) problems to SELECT, MATCH, and FIT. These are operations over different representations, not confidence ranks.

| Operation | Proposed correspondence | Result and scope |
|---|---|---|
| SELECT | A: values, identifiers, predicates | Scoped Fact bound to acquisition conditions |
| MATCH | B: context and semantic representation | Contextual Evidence |
| FIT | C: relationship structure and graphs | Structural Evidence |

This assignment is the school's proposal. A FQuery file-search plugin and an IBD model driver may share an abstract matching operation while remaining separate implementations. A shared name does not establish a shared API, algorithm, guarantee, or completed implementation.

## Begin with open variables

B/C research does not initially fix sender and receiver, causal direction, medium, encoding or quantization requirements, or ontology. Shared experience, environmental cues, hand signals, language, and synchronization can each be investigated. An individual experiment must state its chosen conditions and comparison method.

Historical school terms such as spiritual quantum communication and magical quanta connect to kotodama, religious history, and the history of technology. Do not attribute them to Shannon or Weaver. Their names do not establish or disprove a physical mechanism. Preserve both Shannon's treatment of continuous signals and the open questions of this research.

## A cross-cultural example

Mark 2:27 and the Japanese expression 本末転倒 can be compared through a selected relation between ends and means. A proposed structural correspondence (FIT) does not prove a common historical source (SELECT). Different sources do not independently disprove the structural correspondence.

Likewise, a sensor recording 2°C at location P and time T does not establish that an entire river was 2°C. Keep conclusions within the projection actually compared.

## Retain the evaluation

OAE design requirements include the subject, source revision, Observer, rule, World and fact scope, time, evaluated projection, verdict, limits, unresolved state, and provenance. This is not a claim of a completed universal schema or runtime.

Negative cases include FIT becoming common origin, SELECT mismatch becoming false FIT, a point measurement becoming a global result, and unknown becoming false. Vary inputs and contexts to compare recurrence and stopping behavior.
