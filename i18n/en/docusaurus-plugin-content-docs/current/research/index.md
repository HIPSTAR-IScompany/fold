# An entry point to Infoton Engineering

Infoton Engineering studies how information acquires meaning and effects through observers, contexts, purposes, and transformation routes. Spiritual Engineering provides the design philosophy; Infoton Engineering names the research and engineering domain; Context Engineering is one implementation technique.

## Selection before representation

What was selected for observation, grouped as identical, discarded, or mapped into a representation? Those choices are part of the research. “Before quantization” extends beyond an ADC: it also includes classification and meaning selection before symbolization and representation.

A basic FAM explanation uses ψ for the input, ∇φ for its route, λ for purpose and output, and Q for observation and verification. These are explanatory correspondences; consult implementation sources for function signatures. A different combination can change both the result and whether a connection is possible.

## A small example from the source

Feces may be avoided by a hygiene system, eaten by maggots, used in a child's joke, or converted into fuel by suitable equipment. A textual insult cannot feed a maggot that has no text-interpretation port.

Record which system, purpose, and transformation make an effect possible instead of assigning the object one universal value. An unavailable connection can return ⊥. Supporting that result does not by itself validate every hypothesis: also specify observations that would reject or revise the proposed explanation.

## Design an experiment

Keep the model and input fixed while varying context selection or representation. Record which outputs the author accepts, cultural meaning lost, misrouting, stops, and revision counts. Compare successful preservation of purpose alongside speed. Record the evaluator, observable material, and evaluation rule.

Retain historical terminology, faith, and metaphysical lineage in the source. A crosswalk to another discipline does not establish identity. Continue with [Evidence and OAE](evidence-and-oae.md) for deliberately unbound research conditions.
