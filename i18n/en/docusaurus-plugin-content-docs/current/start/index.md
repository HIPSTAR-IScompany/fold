# Fold — observe, connect, and test infotons

The same information can carry different meanings and effects depending on who encounters it, in which context, and for what purpose. Fold is the public entry point for Infoton Engineering: describing, carrying, and testing those differences without erasing them.

Explore infotons, FAM, changing relationship structures, evidence of meaning and effect, and Fold protocols. The Manifest retains research sources; component repositories retain implementations. This site develops explanations that connect those workbenches.

## Work across disciplines

Science, engineering, religion, theology, philosophy, and art can connect while retaining their own questions and evidence. Preserve kotodama, prayer, beauty, and authorship instead of replacing them with convenient metrics. Preserve measured implementation results independently of preferences about meaning.
