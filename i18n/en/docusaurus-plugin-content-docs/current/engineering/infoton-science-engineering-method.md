# Infoton Science and Infoton Engineering optimize in opposite directions

This is an **en-US editorial reference**, based directly on the [Japanese canonical methodology](https://github.com/saitoomituru/ZeroRoomLab-manifest/blob/54bfe487cd28420caa1589ba64824102dab83f16/docs/theory/infoton-science-engineering-fit-distillation.ja.md). It does not replace implementation contracts, execute a Kernel, or claim a completed ASTRO/AAE runtime.

## Distillation versus implementation

**Infoton Science** proposes a Fit connecting general propositions from established disciplines into an M-dimensional relational structure. It then **distills away unnecessary constraints** until it retains the shortest structure that can still activate the relevant conditions, preserve its explanation scope, and remain falsifiable. The output is the **most plausible candidate structure under the current evidence and assumptions**, not universal truth. “Most plausible” here is a methodological objective, not a report of a numerically calculated likelihood.

**Infoton Engineering** proceeds in the opposite direction. To make purpose λ work in an actual environment, it **adds evidence, instrumentation, adapters, exception handling, redundancy, fallback paths, validation, and recovery procedures**. The operations complement one another: implementation failures go back to the scientific distillation process.

| Infoton Science | Infoton Engineering |
|---|---|
| Distill Fit down to necessary connections | Add sufficient implementation constraints |
| Return reducible propositions to established disciplines | Use standard solvers, adapters, fixtures, and recovery paths |
| Withdraw a Fit when a necessary connection fails | Improve and retest when requirements fail |
| Preserve explanation and falsification scope | Preserve availability, resilience, and operational receipts |

## Two routes to falsification

**On another discipline's desk:** A researcher need only falsify a necessary proposition within their own established scholarly practice. For a declared conjunctive Fit `H = A ∧ B ∧ C`, a scope-valid refutation of one indispensable term can invalidate H. OR alternatives, out-of-scope findings, and mere analogies must not be treated as the same dependency.

**On the Infoton Science desk:** A proposed connection can itself fail type compatibility in meaning, causality, observer context, World boundary, authority, time, or translation interfaces. The method welcomes speculative leaps, but not silent acceptance of mismatched types. An untested connection, an unknown translation, and a proven incompatibility are distinct outcomes. No general-purpose M-dimensional checker is claimed as shipped.

Whenever a substructure can be adequately expressed and tested using an existing linear or nonlinear solver or an established natural, social, or engineering discipline, return that portion to its appropriate field. The research phrase **UnFold (scholarly reduction)** is not the destructive FAM `unFold` operation, nor the non-destructive reference expansion `DeFold`. Keep provenance, identity, revision, and falsification dependencies intact.

## OAE and recurring graph re-tracing

A proposed operating loop uses contemporary **OAE (Observer Agential Effect)** records containing source revisions, observers, rules, world scopes, type checks, and outcomes. A future Cron job could revisit a versioned dynamic dependency graph and propagate newly grounded refutations or corrected sources. This is **not a claim of a deployed continuous audit**. Never backfill missing historical OAEs; retain `historical-oae-unavailable`.

### Example: physical cart abandonment

A shopper may have both money and products available yet be unable to finish purchasing because no safe last-mile transport option is available. Science seeks a shared minimal transaction-completion structure linking online cart abandonment to physical transport failure. Engineering adds stock notices, suitable boxes, secure mounts, substitution and return flows, and measurements. Negative tests target the **necessary links**, not a universal claim about every store or overall inflation.
